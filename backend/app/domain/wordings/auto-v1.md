## 1. How to read this policy

### 1.1 The agreement
This wording, your declarations page, and any endorsements listed on it form the whole agreement
between you and us. A cover applies only where the declarations page shows a limit or a deductible
for it. Liability cover is shown as two numbers, for example 100/300, meaning the most we pay for
bodily injury to one person and the most we pay for all people injured in one accident, in thousands
of dollars.

### 1.2 Words we use
"Your car" means the vehicle shown on the declarations page, a vehicle you acquire during the policy
period and tell us about within thirty days, and a temporary substitute while your car is out of use
for servicing or repair. "You" means the named insured and their spouse or domestic partner living
in the same household. "Accident" means a sudden, unexpected event involving your car that you did
not intend.

## 2. What we cover

### 2.1 Liability to others
We pay damages you are legally liable to pay for bodily injury or property damage arising out of the
use of your car, up to the liability limits on your declarations page. We also defend you against
any claim or suit for such damages, and we pay the cost of that defense in addition to the limits.
We may settle any claim or suit as we consider appropriate.

### 2.2 Collision
Where the declarations page shows a collision deductible, we pay for direct and accidental damage to
your car caused by impact with another vehicle or object, or by your car overturning, regardless of
who was at fault. Payment is the lesser of the cost to repair, the cost to replace, or the actual
cash value of your car immediately before the accident.

### 2.3 Comprehensive
Where the declarations page shows a comprehensive deductible, we pay for direct and accidental loss
to your car that is not a collision, including fire, theft, vandalism, falling objects, hail, flood,
and contact with an animal. Glass damage is settled under this clause.

### 2.4 Medical payments
Where the declarations page shows a medical payments limit, we pay reasonable and necessary medical
and funeral expenses incurred within three years of an accident by you or a passenger in your car,
regardless of fault, up to that limit for each person. This cover pays whether or not another party
is responsible.

### 2.5 Rental reimbursement
Where the declarations page shows a rental limit, we pay for a rental car while your car cannot be
driven because of a loss covered by clause 2.2 or 2.3, up to the daily amount and for the number of
days shown. Payment begins when the car becomes undrivable or is taken for repair, and ends when it
is repaired, replaced, or you accept our settlement.

### 2.6 Towing after a covered loss
We pay reasonable towing and storage charges incurred as a direct result of a loss covered by clause
2.2 or 2.3. Send us the invoice; storage charges run quickly and we settle them from documents, not
estimates.

## 3. What we do not cover

### 3.1 Use we have not agreed to
We do not cover any loss occurring while your car is used to carry people or property for hire,
including ride hailing and delivery work, or while it is being used in a race, speed contest, or
driving instruction for a fee.

### 3.2 Driving without permission or a licence
We do not cover a loss occurring while your car is driven by a person who does not hold a valid
licence for it, or by a person using it without your permission.

### 3.3 Intentional acts and criminal use
We do not cover damage caused on purpose by you or anyone driving with your permission, or a loss
occurring while your car is being used to commit a felony or to flee from law enforcement.

### 3.4 Wear, mechanical breakdown, and tires
We do not cover mechanical or electrical breakdown, wear and tear, rust, or road damage to tires
unless the damage happened in an accident that is otherwise covered.

### 3.5 Personal property in the car
We do not cover personal belongings carried in your car. Those may be covered by a homeowners or
renters policy. We do cover permanently installed equipment forming part of the vehicle.

### 3.6 Fraud and misrepresentation
This policy is void as to any loss where you or anyone acting for you has intentionally concealed or
misrepresented a material fact, made a false statement about the loss, or presented an inflated or
invented claim.

## 4. Your deductible

### 4.1 How the deductible applies
We pay the amount of a covered loss to your car that exceeds the collision or comprehensive
deductible shown on your declarations page, whichever cover applies. The deductible applies once to
each loss. It does not apply to liability cover or to medical payments.

### 4.2 When we recover from someone else
Where we recover our payment from a party responsible for the loss, we return your deductible to you
in the same proportion as our recovery, before we keep anything for ourselves.

## 5. What you must do after an accident

### 5.1 Tell us promptly
You must give us notice of an accident or loss as soon as reasonably possible, and in any event
within ninety days of the date of loss. Where notice is later than that, we may reduce or decline
payment to the extent the delay has prejudiced our ability to investigate.

### 5.2 At the scene
You must give us the other driver's name, contact details, insurer, and vehicle registration where
you were able to obtain them, along with the details of any witnesses. Where police attended, you
must give us the report or incident number. For theft or vandalism, you must report the loss to the
police and give us the report number.

### 5.3 Show us the damage
You must let us inspect and appraise the damaged vehicle before it is repaired or disposed of, and
send us photographs of the vehicles and the scene where you have them, together with a repair
estimate or tow invoice. Do not begin repairs before our inspection unless the vehicle must be moved
to make the road safe.

### 5.4 Cooperate with our investigation
You must cooperate with us, answer reasonable questions about the accident, send us any legal papers
you receive within seven days, and attend an examination under oath if we reasonably request one.
You must not admit liability or agree to settle any claim without our consent.

## 6. How we settle a claim

### 6.1 Repair or total loss
We may pay for repairs, pay the actual cash value of your car, or replace it with one of like kind
and quality. Where the cost to repair exceeds the actual cash value of the car, we treat the loss as
a total loss and pay that value less your deductible and any applicable salvage.

### 6.2 When we pay
We pay within thirty days of agreeing the amount of the loss with you. Where liability is disputed
between drivers, we may pay your own collision claim first and pursue recovery afterwards.

### 6.3 Our right of recovery
Where we pay a claim, we take over your right to recover that amount from anyone responsible for
the loss. You must do nothing after a loss to prejudice that right, and you must help us exercise it
if we ask.

### 6.4 If we disagree on the amount
If you and we disagree on the amount of a loss to your car, either of us may demand an appraisal.
Each side chooses a competent appraiser, the two appraisers choose an umpire, and an agreement by
any two of the three sets the amount. An appraisal decides the amount of a loss, never whether the
loss is covered.
