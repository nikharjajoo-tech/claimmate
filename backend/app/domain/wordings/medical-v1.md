## 1. How to read this policy

### 1.1 The agreement
This wording, your declarations page, and any endorsements listed on it form the whole agreement
between you and us. This policy is supplemental: it pays after your primary health plan, never
instead of it. It is not health insurance and it does not give medical advice or decide what
treatment you should receive.

### 1.2 Words we use
"Your primary health plan" means any health insurance, employer plan, or government program that
covers you before this policy. "Eligible medical costs" means charges for medically necessary
treatment by a licensed provider for a covered condition. "Out of pocket" means the part of an
eligible charge you actually paid yourself and were not reimbursed for by anyone else. "Explanation
of benefits" means the statement your primary health plan issues showing what it allowed, paid, and
left to you.

## 2. What we pay

### 2.1 What we pay
We reimburse eligible medical costs you paid yourself after your primary health plan has paid its
share, up to the annual maximum shown on your declarations page. We pay only amounts shown as your
responsibility on an explanation of benefits from that plan, less the deductible in clause 3.1. We
do not pay providers directly; we reimburse you.

### 2.2 Covered services
Eligible medical costs include office and urgent care visits, emergency department treatment,
diagnostic imaging and laboratory work, prescription drugs dispensed for a covered condition,
outpatient procedures, and medically necessary injections and infusions given as part of that
treatment. Treatment must be received while this policy is in force.

### 2.3 Annual maximum
The annual maximum on your declarations page is the most we pay for all claims in a policy year,
however many claims you make. Amounts you pay towards your deductible do not count against the
annual maximum. When the annual maximum is reached, cover ends for that policy year and resumes at
renewal.

### 2.4 Emergency treatment away from home
Where you receive emergency treatment away from your home area, we reimburse eligible medical costs
on the same basis as treatment at home, including the case where your primary health plan applies a
higher out of network share. You must still send us the explanation of benefits from that plan.

## 3. Your deductible and how amounts are applied

### 3.1 Deductible
You pay the first amount shown as your annual deductible on your declarations page before we
reimburse anything in a policy year. The deductible applies once per policy year, not once per
claim, and it is calculated from the amounts left to you by your primary health plan. We tell you
how much of it remains each time we settle a claim.

### 3.2 How we apply amounts in order
For each claim we take the provider's charge, subtract what your primary health plan allowed and
paid, subtract any part of your annual deductible not yet used, subtract any amount another party
has already reimbursed you, and reimburse what remains up to the annual maximum. Amounts a provider
wrote off or discounted are not costs you paid and are not reimbursed.

## 4. What we do not pay

### 4.1 Cosmetic and elective treatment
We do not pay for cosmetic procedures, elective treatment that is not medically necessary, weight
loss programs, fertility treatment, or experimental treatment that your primary health plan
classifies as investigational.

### 4.2 Services your primary health plan denied
We do not pay for a service your primary health plan denied as not medically necessary, out of
scope, or not a covered benefit of that plan. A denial is not the same as a cost share: where the
explanation of benefits shows the service was allowed but left an amount to you, that amount remains
eligible under clause 2.1. Where a service was denied, tell us if you are appealing, and send us the
outcome when you have it.

### 4.3 Treatment without a primary plan decision
We do not pay for treatment you have not first submitted to your primary health plan, unless that
plan has confirmed in writing that the service is not a benefit it administers. Self-paying at the
time of treatment does not remove this requirement.

### 4.4 Excluded categories
We do not pay for dental or vision care other than treatment of an accidental injury, long term or
custodial care, routine physical examinations required for employment, insurance, or travel, over
the counter products not dispensed on prescription, or missed appointment charges.

### 4.5 Fraud and misrepresentation
This policy is void as to any claim where you or anyone acting for you has intentionally concealed
or misrepresented a material fact, altered a bill or explanation of benefits, or presented an
inflated or invented claim.

## 5. What you must do

### 5.1 Tell us promptly
You must give us notice of a claim as soon as reasonably possible, and in any event within ninety
days of the date of service. Where notice is later than that, we may reduce or decline payment to
the extent the delay has prejudiced our ability to verify the claim.

### 5.2 Use your primary health plan first
You must submit the treatment to your primary health plan before claiming under this policy, and
give that plan the information it needs to process the claim. Where the primary plan is still
processing, tell us: we hold the claim rather than declining it.

### 5.3 Documents we need
Send an itemized bill, your primary plan's explanation of benefits, and proof that you paid. The
itemized bill must show the provider, the dates of service, the services given, and the charges. The
explanation of benefits must cover the same dates and charges. Proof of payment means a receipt,
card statement, or bank record in your name showing the amount you paid.

### 5.4 Answer our questions and let us verify
You must cooperate with us, answer reasonable questions about the treatment and the amounts claimed,
and authorize us to confirm the bill and the explanation of benefits with the provider or the
primary health plan where we need to. We ask only for what is needed to settle the claim.

### 5.5 Tell us about other recoveries
You must tell us about any other insurance, health account, employer arrangement, or third party
that has paid or will pay part of the same costs. Where you are reimbursed twice for the same cost,
you must return the duplicate amount to us.

## 6. How we settle a claim

### 6.1 What we reimburse
We reimburse the amount calculated under clause 3.2, up to the annual maximum on your declarations
page. We reimburse you, in the currency of your declarations page, by the method you nominate.

### 6.2 When we pay
We pay within thirty days of receiving the documents listed in clause 5.3 and agreeing the amount
with you. Where one date of service is fully documented and another is not, we pay the documented
one without waiting for the rest.

### 6.3 If we cannot agree
If you disagree with how we have settled a claim, you may ask us to review it and send any further
documents within sixty days. A review considers the amount and the documents; where you believe a
service was wrongly denied by your primary health plan, that appeal is made to that plan, not to us.
