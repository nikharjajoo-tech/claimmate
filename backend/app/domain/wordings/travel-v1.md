## 1. How to read this policy

### 1.1 The agreement
This wording, your declarations page, and any endorsements listed on it form the whole agreement
between you and us. This policy covers one trip, between the dates shown on your declarations page.
An event that happens before the start date or after the end date is not covered, whatever its
cause.

### 1.2 Words we use
"The trip" means the journey described on your declarations page, from the moment you leave home to
the moment you return. "Prepaid nonrefundable expenses" means amounts you paid before the trip that
the provider will not refund and has not credited. "Common carrier" means an airline, train, ferry,
or coach operator licensed to carry passengers for hire. "Covered reason" means one of the reasons
listed in clause 2.2.

## 2. What we cover

### 2.1 Trip cancellation
Where you cancel the trip before departure for a covered reason, we reimburse your prepaid
nonrefundable expenses up to the cancellation limit shown on your declarations page. You must cancel
the booking with the provider as soon as you know you cannot travel; we do not pay any additional
amount that accrues because you delayed.

### 2.2 Covered reasons for cancellation
A covered reason is: sudden illness, injury, or death of you, a travelling companion, or an
immediate family member, certified by a doctor; a named storm, earthquake, flood, or fire that makes
your destination accommodation uninhabitable or closes the destination airport; the cancellation of
your flight or other common carrier service by the carrier; jury service or a court summons you
cannot defer; or the total loss of your home by fire or flood within fourteen days of departure.

### 2.3 Trip delay
Where your departure is delayed for six hours or more by a covered reason, we reimburse reasonable
additional costs for meals, accommodation, and local transport, up to the daily amount shown on your
declarations page and for the number of days shown. The delay is measured from the scheduled
departure time to the actual departure time of the first available replacement service.

### 2.4 Trip interruption
Where the trip is cut short after departure for a covered reason, we reimburse the unused portion of
your prepaid nonrefundable expenses and the reasonable cost of returning home by the most direct
route. We do not pay for an upgrade in class of travel compared with your original booking.

### 2.5 Baggage delay
Where your checked baggage is delayed by a common carrier for twelve hours or more on the outward
journey, we reimburse the reasonable cost of essential replacement clothing and toiletries bought
during the delay, up to the baggage delay limit shown on your declarations page. Keep the receipts
and the carrier's delay report; this cover settles from documents, not estimates.

### 2.6 Baggage loss and damage
Where your baggage is lost, stolen, or damaged during the trip, we pay the actual cash value of the
items, up to the baggage limit shown on your declarations page and up to $300 for any one item. We
pay only the amount the common carrier or any other insurer has not already paid you.

## 3. What we do not cover

### 3.1 Events outside the trip dates
We do not cover any cancellation, delay, interruption, or loss arising from an event that occurs
outside the trip dates shown on your declarations page, or from a booking made after the event
became foreseeable.

### 3.2 Known events and pre-existing conditions
We do not cover a cancellation caused by an event that was public knowledge or reasonably
foreseeable when you bought this policy, including a storm already named, a strike already
announced, or a travel advisory already published. We do not cover a medical condition for which you
received treatment, diagnosis, or a change of medication in the ninety days before you bought this
policy.

### 3.3 Change of mind and work commitments
We do not cover cancellation because you changed your mind, because the trip became unaffordable, or
because of work commitments, unless your employer terminated your employment after you bought this
policy.

### 3.4 Refunds and compensation received
We do not pay for any amount that has been refunded, credited, vouchered, or compensated by the
carrier, the accommodation provider, a tour operator, a credit card scheme, or another insurer. You
must tell us what you have recovered elsewhere before we settle.

### 3.5 Alcohol, drugs, and unlawful acts
We do not cover any loss arising while you are under the influence of alcohol or drugs not
prescribed to you, or from your participation in an unlawful act.

### 3.6 Valuables and unattended property
We do not cover cash, travel documents, jewelry, or electronics that were in checked baggage rather
than carried with you, or any property left unattended in a public place.

## 4. Your deductible

### 4.1 How the deductible applies
Where your declarations page shows a deductible, we pay the amount of a covered loss that exceeds
it. The deductible applies once to each claim, not to each receipt or each item.

## 5. What you must do

### 5.1 Tell us promptly
You must give us notice of a claim as soon as reasonably possible, and in any event within ninety
days of the date of the event. Where notice is later than that, we may reduce or decline payment to
the extent the delay has prejudiced our ability to investigate.

### 5.2 Get it in writing from the carrier
You must obtain and send us the carrier's written confirmation of the cancellation, delay, or
baggage incident, showing the scheduled and actual times and the reason given. For baggage, this
means the property irregularity report issued at the airport.

### 5.3 Send us the trip documents
You must send us your original itinerary and booking confirmation, receipts for the prepaid
nonrefundable expenses you claim, receipts for any additional costs you incurred, and documentation
of any refund, voucher, or credit you received. Where you claim for illness, you must send us a
doctor's certificate covering the dates in question.

### 5.4 Keep your losses reasonable
You must take reasonable steps to limit your costs, including accepting a reasonable alternative
service offered by the carrier. We do not pay costs you could have avoided.

### 5.5 Fraud and misrepresentation
This policy is void as to any claim where you or anyone acting for you has intentionally concealed
or misrepresented a material fact, made a false statement about the claim, or presented an inflated
or invented claim.

## 6. How we settle a claim

### 6.1 What we pay
We reimburse documented amounts you actually paid and have not recovered elsewhere, up to the limits
shown on your declarations page. We settle in the currency of your declarations page, converting
foreign costs at the rate applying on the date you paid them.

### 6.2 When we pay
We pay within thirty days of receiving the documents listed in part 5 and agreeing the amount with
you. Where one part of a claim is documented and another is not, we pay the documented part without
waiting.

### 6.3 Recovery from others
Where we pay a claim, we take over your right to recover that amount from the carrier or any other
party responsible. You must tell us about any compensation you receive after we have paid, and
return any amount you recover twice.
